## Notes

- Positions are rebuilt from each fold's parameters over contiguous test windows, so a parameter change at a fold boundary is traded and charged. The walk-forward itself drops each fold's first bar and that switch, which is why its leg Sharpes differ slightly (printed at the top of the run).
- The static benchmark knows the strategy's average exposure in advance. That favours the benchmark a little; it is the price of asking only about timing.
